# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## About the Series
**Stranger Things** is a science fiction and horror series created by the Duffer Brothers.

## Why I Like It
I enjoy this series because of its interesting storyline, memorable characters, and unexpected plot twists.

## Favorite Characters
- Eleven
- Mike Wheeler
- Dustin Henderson
- Steve Harrington

## Favorite Quote

## Rating
**9/10** – I recommend this series to people who enjoy mystery, adventure, and science fiction.
